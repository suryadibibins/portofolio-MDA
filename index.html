Zomato Business Analysis

An analysis of Zomato transaction and restaurant data to evaluate business performance, identify key sales contributors, and assess data reliability.

Background

  • Zomato is an online food-delivery platform whose business performance can be evaluated through customer transactions, restaurant contribution, and sales trends.
  • The analysis combines five related datasets: Users, Orders, Restaurants, Menu, and Food, enabling performance reviews across multiple business dimensions.

Objective

  • Evaluate overall performance using Total Sales, Total Orders, and Average Order Value (AOV).
  • Identify sales and order patterns across time, cities, restaurants, customers, and cuisines, while checking unusual patterns that may affect interpretation.

Analysis Process

  • Prepared and connected the five datasets, then conducted data exploration and validation using Python (Pandas).
  • Used SQL to calculate KPIs and compare performance across business dimensions.
  • Developed Tableau views for monthly trends, city and restaurant contribution, day type, cuisine, and rating segments.
  • Reviewed unusual results, especially the weekday/weekend order distribution, before interpreting them as business patterns.

Insight

  • The dataset recorded approximately 987M in sales from 150,281 orders, with an Average Order Value of approximately 6,565.
  • Tirupati was the leading city by sales (approximately 43M), while Domino’s Pizza led the restaurant comparison (approximately 5.03M).
  • Restaurants without ratings contributed the largest sales share; rating data is a limitation because a substantial proportion of rating records is missing.
  • Weekend transactions represented only 538 orders (0.36%), an unusually low share that requires validation before drawing operational conclusions.

Recommendation

  • Monitor sales and order trends over time to identify periods of declining transaction activity.
  • Review customer and restaurant contributions to identify retention opportunities and performance benchmarks.
  • Investigate missing restaurant ratings and improve feedback-data completeness where possible.
  • Validate date fields and weekday/weekend classification before using day-type patterns for staffing or promotional decisions.
  • Use city-level performance comparisons to guide further market and restaurant reviews.

FMCG / Retail Sales Analysis

An FMCG/retail sales analysis project examining transaction performance, sales and profit trends, and comparisons across product categories and regions.

Background

  • The project evaluates FMCG/retail transaction performance through sales and profit trends.
  • Performance is examined across time periods, product categories, and regions to understand how different business dimensions contribute to overall results.

Objective

  • Assess how sales and profit change over time.
  • Compare category and regional performance to identify segments that warrant further investigation.
  • Evaluate sales and profitability together to avoid treating high revenue as an automatic indicator of strong business performance.

Analysis Process

  • Prepared and cleaned transaction data, then reviewed data quality before conducting the analysis.
  • Analyzed sales and profit trends across the available time periods.
  • Compared performance by product category and region to identify differences in business contribution.
  • Used spreadsheet-based analysis and the documented A/B testing component where applicable.
  • Presented comparisons through visualizations to make performance patterns easier to interpret.

Insight

  • The analysis evaluates sales and profit performance across time periods, product categories, and regions.
  • Comparing sales with profit provides a more complete view of performance because higher sales do not necessarily translate into higher profitability.
  • The available project summary does not specify verified numerical results or identify the highest- and lowest-performing segments; these should be taken directly from the project analysis/dashboard rather than assumed.

Recommendation

  • Monitor sales and profit trends together to identify changes in business performance.
  • Evaluate product categories using both sales contribution and profitability, not sales alone.
  • Review regional differences to identify where additional investigation may be needed.
  • Confirm the drivers behind category and regional performance before setting priorities or proposing specific commercial actions.

Hospital Data Analysis

A healthcare analytics project using operational and financial indicators to understand hospital activity and support data-informed management decisions.

Background

  • The project analyzes hospital operations and financial performance to provide management with a clearer view of patient activity and service performance.
  • The dataset contains four tables: Admissions (5,000 records), Billing (5,000), Patients (1,800), and Doctors (64).

Objective

  • Understand patient demand, admission activity, and disease distribution.
  • Review doctor activity and hospital revenue to support operational performance monitoring.
  • Provide analytical input for capacity planning and medicine procurement, subject to validation against actual findings.

Analysis Process

  • Reviewed the patient, admission, doctor, and billing datasets and related records through their corresponding identifiers.
  • Analyzed patient and admission volumes, disease patterns, doctor activity, and financial performance.
  • Built a Tableau Public dashboard using Total Patients, Total Admissions, Total Net Revenue, Revenue per Admission, and Average Length of Stay.
  • Used the dashboard indicators to examine operational activity alongside financial performance.

Insight

  • The dashboard brings together patient demand, admission activity, disease distribution, doctor activity, and revenue performance.
  • Total Patients and Total Admissions provide complementary views of patient reach and service activity.
  • Total Net Revenue and Revenue per Admission provide financial perspectives, while Average Length of Stay adds an operational measure.
  • The project information available for this portfolio does not include verified numerical findings or a confirmed leading diagnosis/doctor; those details should be added only from the dashboard results.

Recommendation

  • Use patient-volume and admission trends as supporting evidence when reviewing service capacity.
  • Consider disease-distribution patterns when reviewing medicine inventory and procurement requirements.
  • Review doctor activity alongside patient and admission volumes when assessing workload.
  • Monitor revenue, Revenue per Admission, and Average Length of Stay together to avoid interpreting any single KPI in isolation.
  • Confirm detailed dashboard findings before setting specific staffing, capacity, or procurement targets.

RevoGrocers

A sales-data preparation project focused on data quality checks and validation in BigQuery before the dataset is used for reliable business analysis.

Background

  • RevoGrocers is a sales-performance data project centered on preparing transaction data for subsequent analysis.
  • Reliable sales reporting depends on consistent data types, standardized categories, and appropriate handling of missing or duplicate records.

Objective

  • Identify data-quality issues that could affect the accuracy of downstream sales analysis.
  • Assess consistency across data types, spelling, missing values, duplicate records, and categorical values.
  • Prepare a consistent and validated dataset as a foundation for further exploratory and sales-performance analysis.

Analysis Process

  • Used Google BigQuery to inspect the dataset structure and column data types.
  • Checked for misspelled or inconsistent values and reviewed category consistency.
  • Identified missing values and duplicate records that could affect analysis.
  • Validated query outputs and reviewed data consistency before proceeding to further analysis.

Insight

  • The documented workflow covers key data-quality dimensions: data types, spelling consistency, missing values, duplicate records, and category consistency.
  • These checks help identify issues that may distort KPI calculations, category comparisons, and subsequent business conclusions.
  • The available project summary does not provide verified counts for each issue; quantified results should be added from the actual BigQuery query outputs.

Recommendation

  • Resolve or document data-quality issues before using the dataset for KPI reporting.
  • Standardize categorical values and data formats to support consistent grouping and comparison.
  • Define and document how missing values and duplicate records are treated.
  • Retain repeatable validation queries as part of the data-preparation workflow.
  • Proceed to sales-performance analysis after the cleaned dataset has been validated.
