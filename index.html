Business Performance

Zomato Business Analysis

A business analysis project focused on understanding performance patterns, assessing data quality, and translating findings into business-oriented insights.

Background

  • Zomato is an online food-delivery business where performance can be assessed through customer orders, restaurant contribution, and sales trends.
  • The analysis uses connected Users, Orders, Restaurants, Menu, and Food data to understand business performance.

Objective

  • Measure overall sales performance using Total Sales, Total Orders, and Average Order Value (AOV).
  • Identify sales and order patterns across time, cities, restaurants, customers, and cuisine.

Analysis Process

  • Prepare and connect the five data tables, then conduct exploratory analysis using Python/Pandas and SQL-based analysis.
  • Build KPI and performance views in Tableau, including monthly trends, city and restaurant contribution, day type, cuisine, and rating segments.
  • Review unusual results—particularly the weekday/weekend order distribution—before treating them as business patterns.

Insight

  • Recorded overall performance: approximately 987M in sales, 150,281 orders, and 6,565 AOV.
  • Tirupati was the leading city by sales (approximately 43M), while Domino's Pizza led restaurant sales (approximately 5.03M).
  • Unrated restaurants contributed the largest sales share; the weekday/weekend split was highly imbalanced and requires validation.

Recommendation

  • Investigate the high sales contribution from unrated restaurants and assess whether rating coverage or customer feedback collection needs improvement.
  • Validate date and day-type classification behind the weekday/weekend imbalance before using it for staffing or promotion decisions.
  • Prioritize city- and restaurant-level performance reviews using the dashboard.

Hospital Data Analysis

A healthcare analytics project using operational and financial indicators to monitor hospital activity and support decision-making.

Background

  • The project analyzes hospital operations and financial performance to support hospital management.
  • The dataset contains four tables: admissions (5,000 records), billing (5,000), patients (1,800), and doctors (64).

Objective

  • Understand patient demand, admission activity, disease patterns, and doctor requirements.
  • Monitor hospital revenue and provide analytical input for operational planning, including medicine procurement.

Analysis Process

  • Relate patient, admission, doctor, and billing records through their corresponding identifiers.
  • Analyze patient and admission volumes, disease patterns, doctor activity, and financial performance.
  • Build the dashboard in Tableau Public using Total Patients, Total Admissions, Total Net Revenue, Revenue per Admission, and Average Length of Stay.

Insight

  • The dashboard is designed to reveal patient and admission demand, disease distribution, doctor utilization, and revenue performance.
  • The project information currently available does not include verified numerical findings or a named leading diagnosis/doctor.

Recommendation

  • Use patient-volume and disease-pattern views as supporting evidence for capacity and medicine-procurement planning.
  • Use admission, length-of-stay, and revenue indicators together when reviewing operational performance.
  • Confirm the detailed dashboard results before setting specific staffing or procurement targets.
